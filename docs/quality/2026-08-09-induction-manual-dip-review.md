## What was reviewed

This review covers the Induction Manual profile itself. Resume remains the benchmark for information-contract depth and the existing strict 12-point DIP review remains the pass gate.

The hand-authored contract covers welcome and organisation context; how work is performed; policies and safety; and systems, contacts and first-period guidance. Required facts include organisation, role context, organisation context, values, core systems, communication channels, standard workflows, key policies, safety requirements, manager contact, support contacts and first-period priorities.

Every required fact has the same nine-decision definition depth as Resume.

## Profile-specific findings

### Organisation context must be real

TED cannot invent mission, values, history, customers or internal culture because those details make an induction manual sound convincing. Where values are missing, the contract omits them rather than manufacturing corporate language.

### Systems and ways of working

Named systems, tools, communication channels and meeting routines are operational facts. They must come from confirmed context or supplied source material.

### Safety content

Emergency procedures, evacuation instructions, reporting contacts, PPE and site safety rules are high-consequence content. TED may point to confirmed safety material but cannot invent procedures.

### Policies

Only policies that actually exist and have been supplied or confirmed may be named. Generic policy categories are not silently converted into fictional internal documents.

### Manager and support contacts

The exact manager name can safely remain unresolved using role-based wording. HR, IT and support contacts can likewise use established support-channel wording until names or addresses are confirmed.

### First-period priorities

First-week and first-month expectations are role-specific facts, not generic onboarding filler. They remain interactive until supplied.

## Placeholder and clarification behaviour

Organisation, role and manager facts may be reused where intentionally shared. Systems, policies, safety requirements and priorities remain independent so resolving one cannot accidentally rewrite another part of the manual.

## Zero-blank determination

All induction sections remain usable when facts are incomplete. Missing organisation-specific detail becomes an interactive placeholder or approved neutral wording rather than an empty section.

## Pass decision

The Induction Manual profile passes the existing strict 12-point gate only if its hand-authored contract continues to match Resume depth, contains no fabricated organisation or safety content, and passes the all-facts-missing non-blank tests plus full repository CI.
