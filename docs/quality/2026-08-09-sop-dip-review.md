## What was reviewed

This review assesses the SOP profile itself. The shared strict 12-point DIP review remains the canonical pass gate and is not duplicated here.

The SOP contract is hand-authored around executable procedure logic: purpose/scope/prerequisites, ordered steps and controls, and roles/records/review. Required facts include process name, purpose, scope, prerequisites, ordered steps, step owners, decision points, completion standard, process owner, record location, and review trigger.

Every required fact contains the complete nine-decision DIP definition.

## Profile-specific findings

### Ordered steps are substantive facts

TED must never manufacture a plausible-looking step simply to make an SOP feel complete. If a step is unknown, the procedure still renders around an interactive placeholder at the correct position.

### Step ownership

A task may be clear while its owner is unknown. That is not a reason to blank or discard the step. The role remains independently resolvable.

### Prerequisites and access

Tools, credentials, licences, systems, PPE, training and access requirements are not inferred from the process title. They are only included when confirmed.

### Safety instructions

Safety warnings are high-consequence operational content. TED cannot improvise them. Where safety information is required but missing, the profile asks for the exact instruction or source rather than inventing protective measures.

### Decision branches

A linear procedure must remain linear unless a real branch is supplied. The profile does not create artificial decision trees for the sake of apparent sophistication.

### Completion standard

The SOP must say how correct completion is recognised. This is export-critical because an ordered list without a completion standard can still leave the operator unsure whether the procedure succeeded.

### Records and review

Recordkeeping systems, retention locations and review frequencies are optional unless supplied. Neutral wording may acknowledge applicable recordkeeping without inventing a storage platform or retention period.

## Placeholder and clarification behaviour

Process identity, steps, owners and completion criteria use separate semantic placeholders. Shared resolution is limited to intentionally repeated facts such as the process name or owner role. Resolving one missing step cannot alter another step or unrelated control.

## Zero-blank determination

Even when every operational detail is missing, the SOP can still render its full procedural structure with interactive placeholders and confirmed connective wording. Missing operational facts are therefore unresolved inputs, not empty-section conditions.

## Pass decision

The SOP profile passes the existing strict 12-point DIP review because the contract preserves executable structure, blocks invented operational facts and supports section-level completion under the Resume benchmark. Full repository CI remains required release evidence.
