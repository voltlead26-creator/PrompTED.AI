`section-designer.ts` now checks the exact completed design shape, ordered section count, genuine required flags, bounded nonempty facts, allowed domain/structure and unique keys within the existing 80-character wire contract. Valid keys, requiredness and cautious advice policy remain. Invalid completed output returns null; provider/accounting errors retain their original identity. Cancellation is checked before dispatch and before accepting the result. `BESPOKE_DESIGN_VALIDATOR_VERSION` is bound into new bespoke allowance policy by the generation admission integration.

`allowance-reservations.ts` recognises the four additional exact reconciliation codes emitted by the existing router, including local-fallback uncertainty and capacity/dispatch acknowledgement. Arbitrary strings do not acquire recovery meaning. `provider-router.ts` now records an unresolved dispatch permission as an unknown/ambiguous attempt and keeps capacity in reconciliation, even when this worker made no HTTP request. This preserves uncertainty; it is not evidence that a second provider charge previously occurred.
